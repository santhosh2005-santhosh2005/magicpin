How does magicpin AI filter out WhatsApp auto-replies? +
Vera detects standardized WhatsApp Business greeting templates ("Thank you for contacting us...", "We will get back soon") and duplicate responses, immediately terminating the turn to prevent bot-to-bot cost loops.
How does the single binary commitment (Reply YES) work? +
Instead of presenting confusing multi-choice questionnaires, Vera does all the legwork (drafting offers, compiling search figures) and asks for one simple binary action: "Reply YES to publish".
